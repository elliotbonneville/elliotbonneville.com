Build update · September 13, 2026

All 26 selected mechanical lines are ordered. The order audit found no SKU or quantity shortage against the selected list. Radiator stands are included. Physical receipt, fit, loaded rigidity and cooling remain unverified.

What remains

  • PSU mounts: replacement mounts are still unselected. Bracket shapes in the model reserve space; they do not specify an approved mount.
  • Tools and supplies: check existing tools against the practical checklist. The assembly method calls for removable Loctite 222MS and SF 7649 activator on twelve switch/radiator nuts. The M5 shelf nuts already have nylon locking inserts.
  • Power and startup: the auxiliary harness, load allocation and startup sequence for the two owned supplies still need qualification.
