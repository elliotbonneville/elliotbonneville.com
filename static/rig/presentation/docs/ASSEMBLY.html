Assembly and adjustment — technical reference

Start with the beginner build manual for ordered sessions, part labels and hardware bags. This page retains the dimensional stack analysis.

Planning guide; physical fit, restraint and loaded cooling remain unverified. The current central-opening clamp has nominal mesh clearance, replacing the earlier unverified edge-slot arrangement. Complete the remaining host, shelf, PSU and radiator attachments before moving equipment.

Retain the CPU cooling system

The confirmed SilverStone XE360-TR5 must be completely installed for operation, with its pump and radiator fans connected. For the solo transfer, follow TRANSFER.md: the proposed sequence removes and remounts the cold plate so the motherboard and radiator can be carried separately. The Vornado stand fan supplements airflow around the rack. It cannot replace the CPU cooler. Secure the radiator, protect its tubes from kinks/pulls, and check the pump/radiator installation instructions. Do not power the CPU with its cooler removed.

Level and square the eventual frame

  1. Level and stabilize the rack using its manufacturer provisions. Attach the shelf at matching rack heights on all four rails. Check the deck for rocking or twist with a straightedge; do not assume perforated steel is perfectly flat.
  2. Assemble the empty frame on a flat bench. Keep joints snug initially. Use a square and compare diagonals. Use the selected SEP kits' post-assembly spring nuts through the slot opening; the spring holds the nut while starting the screw. Verify actual engagement.
  3. Set paired adapter rails coplanar using a straightedge and equal setup blocks. Measure heights from the same base datum. Match heights at both ends of the single eight-card row. A spirit level does not establish PCIe mating height.
  4. Install the two top bars and four flat corner plates, then tighten progressively while rechecking squareness. Use selected hardware torque guidance; do not apply an M5 torque to M3 PCB screws.
  5. Position the frame over the actual shelf vents. Use positive attachment with appropriate bearing/backing. If required positions cannot use vents, the user permits drilling. Mark only from actual fit, with electronics removed; deburr and clean before reinstalling them. Do not fabricate a vent map from the render.
  6. Dry-fit one GPU and adapter with power disconnected and the GPU independently supported. Confirm PCIe key orientation and full seating. Bring the rear support and central-opening bracket clamp to the seated card, not the reverse.
  7. Repeat across the row, distributing the load along the frame. Verify that tightening a support does not bow the adapter or lift the edge connector.

Useful tools: square, straightedge, caliper, matched setup blocks, small clamps, feeler gauges, proper hex drivers, and rigid shims. Never pull a twisted assembly flat with its mounting screws or use a PCB as the alignment jig.

Corrected adapter supports

Each adapter has two main mounting holes at 100 mm pitch. The initial CAD stack is two 0.5 mm M3 DIN 125 washers (7 mm OD) beneath a 30 mm Harwin R30-3003002 body. The support height is 31 mm; its separate 6 mm male stud is below the body. There are sixteen bodies and thirty-two base washers for eight adapters.

Use HNTF5-3 nuts in matching HFS5 slots. Based on nominal slot/nut dimensions, a 6±0.2 mm stud and two 0.4–0.6 mm washers give 4.6–5.4 mm stud projection: ~3.4–4.1 mm geometric overlap and ≥0.6 mm nominal floor reserve. This excludes unspecified nut/profile tolerances and thread-end chamfers. Verify clamping on one actual assembly. The earlier 9 mm adjacent-nut-fit claim is irrelevant: the actual adapter has no such pair of holes.

Nominal top fastener: M3x6 low-head screw, 0.5 mm washer, 1.6 mm PCB. Nominal female engagement is 3.9 mm against the standoff's ≥6 mm threaded depth. Add small rigid shims above the body for measured fit; change the top screw length when necessary and check bottoming. Keep matched support heights, no springs. The 7 mm base washer is needed because the body alone does not span a 6 mm slot.

Whole-tier ±5 mm vertical movement remains a geometric setup allowance, performed unloaded. All adapter and GPU supports belonging to that tier move together and are reclamped. Slot travel cannot repair the wrong hole pattern, wrong key orientation, bad cut length or an incompatible bracket clamp.

PCIe connections and cooling check

GPU → its own C-Payne x16 slot → two MCIO 8i cables → one PM50100 → two MCIO 8i cables → a motherboard host retimer. Four GPUs share each switch's upstream x16 link. The switch never physically supports a GPU.

Use Expose PCIe sockets to inspect the adapters and cables. The corrected viewer retains the actual GPU PCB in the normal assembled view. The adapter rotates 180° from the rejected draft to align its key.

After the mechanical and power interfaces are complete, commission progressively with the Vornado delivering fresh air to the eight-card row, while retaining all component fans. Check actual inlets, backplate/memory cooling, GPU/CPU power and thermal limiting under representative simultaneous load. No loaded cooling result has been obtained from this future rack layout.

Central-opening GPU clamp

Use the large central oval opening, not either narrow outer slot. Nominally: M3×16 head below a 0.5 mm washer, bracket fold, 0.5 mm washer, 9 mm R30-6010902 spacer, then another 0.5 mm washer against the rail and an inverted HNTF5-3 nut. That last washer prevents the narrow spacer from entering the extrusion slot. There is one clamp per card. The rear support remains necessary.

Set rail bottom 60.48 mm above the GPU placement datum as a starting dimension; the actual fully seated GPU and measured stack set final height. Nut overlap is 3.4 mm nominal. The 9 mm spacer's specified ±0.10 mm and three washers each 0.4–0.6 mm give 3.0–3.8 mm overlap with nominal screw, bracket and nut dimensions; those latter tolerances are not included. Check screw bottoming and washer bearing without bowing the bracket or adapter. Do not infer torque from CAD. The active frame has four 220 mm posts. Set support height from the seated card; do not use the historical two-tier post dimensions.

Switch mounting stack

SWITCH-MOUNT.md replaces the floating 7 mm supports with a complete candidate: top screw/washer, PCB, 7 mm male/female body, 9 mm wide washer, shelf, second wide washer, M3 hex nut. Nominal switch placement rises 0.422 mm to seat the reference PCB. Check the actual vent centres and sheet thickness before using this stack. A thicker locking nut will not fit the same 6 mm stud by assumption. Cable restraint and loosening resistance remain required; the reference geometry alone does not settle either.
