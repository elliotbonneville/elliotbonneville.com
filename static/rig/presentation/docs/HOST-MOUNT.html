Adjustable host mounting — September 7, 2026

Use stock HFS5-2020 rails and catalogue fasteners. The motherboard mounts on eleven 20 mm M3 standoffs, each secured to a sliding M3 slot nut. Four longitudinal rails slide across two transverse end beams. The rails set one coordinate; the eleven nuts set the other. No custom tray, drilled extrusion or supplier inquiry.

ASRock's installation notice identifies eleven mounting holes and requires removal of unmatched standoffs. Its illustration shows four rows across the board. Displayed positions are diagram-derived setup targets, not measured hole coordinates or a drilling template. Adjust the empty support assembly to the actual holes; never force the PCB into alignment. The mounting method does not depend on ordering hole-specific parts.

Structure and clearance

The eight rail-to-end-beam angles occupy the open corner outside the left face of each longitudinal rail, facing inward from the end beam. Both angle plates must remain outside the extrusion bodies; their screws enter the side slots of the rail and end beam. The September 8 correction reverses the rail joint's lateral direction, which had incorrectly placed the angle inside the rail. It changes no cuts, quantities or mounting-hole targets.

  • Four 350 mm longitudinal support rails, between two 400 mm end beams.
  • Four 150 mm legs; platform top 170 mm above the shelf, with 48.1 mm nominal clearance over the existing 101.9 mm PSU/foot envelope.
  • Two 125 mm host-card retention posts mounted on the rear end beam.
  • Twenty HBLSS5-SEP angle kits: twelve platform joints, four shelf feet, two retention-post bases, and two height-adjustable card clamps.
  • Installed host shelf is U11; GPU shelf is U18; the empty bottom shelf is U4. Shelf-top separation becomes 311.15 mm; highest host posts are 295 mm above the host shelf, leaving 16.15 mm nominal clearance to the GPU shelf surface. Subtract actual underside protrusions when installing the shelves.
  • Host footprint is 400 × 390 mm before small bracket/screw projections.

This replaces the three 360 mm rails, two 330 mm beams and four 106 mm legs. The GPU frame cuts do not change. Stock tray candidates were investigated; the sliding rails retain more adjustment without another supplier or tray clamps.

Complete motherboard fastener stack

Each support, bottom to top: HNTF5-3 nut in the rail, two 0.5 mm M3 washers, Harwin R30-3002002 20 mm male/female standoff, motherboard, M3×6 low-head screw. The washers span the 6 mm slot. The PCB underside is 21 mm above the rail; only standoff tops may contact the intended motherboard mounting pads. No loose metal, extra unused standoff or metal tray touches the PCB underside.

Harwin's drawing specifies the 6 mm male stud and at least 6 mm usable female thread for this length. With 1 mm base washers, stud projection is 5 mm, matching the previously checked adapter slot-nut stack. M3×6 screw engagement is 3.5 mm with the modeled 2.5 mm PCB, or 4.4 mm for a 1.6 mm PCB; check the delivered board and screw seating. Use no top washer unless its diameter clears nearby parts and engagement is rechecked. Do not torque these screws as structural M5 joints.

Buy fifteen standoffs for eleven installed. Add eleven M3 slot nuts to the combined requirement (35 needed, 40 purchased), eleven M3×6 screws (35 needed, two packs of 25), and 22 base washers. The two host-card clamps also consume four M3 washers, two M3×16 screws and two M3 hex nuts, from the shared packs. Combined M3 standard washers: 106 needed, two packs of 100.

PCIe host-card retention

Each retimer gets its own 125 mm vertical post behind the motherboard, a normal angle at the post base, and a second angle that slides vertically. The retimer's metal mounting tab rests on that second angle. An M3×16 screw, washer above the tab, washer below the angle, and M3 hex nut clamp the tab. The upper angle uses one included M5 screw/nut pair into the post; its second kit pair is spare. The post base slides across the rear beam to align with the card tab.

The vendor Astera CAD has a low-profile bracket: its mounting-tab underside is local Y=33.722 mm, with a usable slot axis at local X=17.1, Z=95.3 mm. These values come from the original host-retimer.obj, not the old fallback's invented full-height tab. Board position along the rails can be adjusted to match the rear beam, and clamp height follows a fully seated card. Restrain cables to the frame so they do not pull on the slot or retimer. Delivered bracket revision and seated contact remain normal assembly checks.

Assembly sequence

  1. Assemble and square the empty platform; leave support rails adjustable.
  2. Locate all eleven holes on the disconnected board. Position the four rails and eleven standoffs to match, without any unmatched support underneath.
  3. Tighten the rail joints and standoff bases with the board removed. Check all support tops with a straightedge, then lower the board and start every M3 screw by hand. Stop if any screw needs to pull the board sideways or downward.
  4. Install the retimers fully, bring their support angles up to the metal tabs, then tighten the angle/post joints and tab clamps without lifting the cards.
  5. Set the shelves at the stated separation and check actual overhead clearance, PSU clearance, cables, cooler tubes and shelf anchors before power-on.

This resolves the motherboard/host-card mounting architecture and its selected hardware. Radiator supports are specified in RADIATOR-MOUNT.md. PSU feet/restraints and shelf-vent alignment remain separate incomplete interfaces; this is not a release of the entire rig kit.
