Rear GPU supports — standard silicone pads

The catalogue lists -80°F to 450°F for this adhesive-backed product. Separate adhesive temperature and thickness/compression tolerances are not given on the product page; do not carry over the old sheet's adhesive specification.

The strip is already the required 12.7 mm width. Cut eight 40 mm lengths with scissors on a suitable surface; 320 mm of its nominal 914.4 mm length is used, leaving ample spare material and an additional trial pad. Apply adhesive to the clean rail, not to the GPU. The front clamp supplies retention; the pad cushions the rear shroud's bearing on the rail and is not a structural adhesive bond to the GPU.

Corrected seating geometry

Clipping all original GPU reference triangles to local x=[-20,20], z=[-106.35,-93.65] finds the lowest plane at native Y=5.67, hence local y=−42.03. Coplanar shroud regions total about 232.029 mm² within the footprint. The whole footprint does not contact the curved underside. The surface belongs to A_COVER_B6000_PG153, not a PCB or fan. The former −41.6 support plane intruded by 0.43 mm. Independent evidence records the mesh hash and method.

The nominal pad is 25.4/16=1.5875 mm thick. Rear rail top is therefore GPU origin Y − 42.03 − 1.5875. Compared with the previous 2.2 mm pad and incorrect shroud datum, the metal rail rises 0.1825 mm, while pad top drops 0.43 mm. GPU positions, card gaps, tier spacing and all extrusion cut lengths remain unchanged. The CAD now shows eight discrete pads rather than two unassigned full-length strips.

Assembly adjustment

Seat and support the GPU/adapter stack first. Leave the rear crossrail's two end joints loose enough to slide vertically. Raise the padded rail evenly until both rear shroud contact regions bear without lifting or twisting the card; then tighten its specified joints. Do not tighten the GPU clamp to force the card down to an incorrectly positioned rail. Check all eight cards along the row for contact; do not force one common rail to compensate for unequal card or support heights. Reposition or shim individual pads if delivered parts require it, then recheck seating. Rigid shim material and thickness must be selected before using that fallback; do not improvise with loose scraps. Record any added shim before changing CAD.

Rubber is not a precision height datum. Contact-based setup accommodates actual strip/adhesive thickness and small compression; no assumed compression percentage has been built into the dimensions. The catalog's material properties and the reference contact area do not establish complete shroud load capacity or long-term adhesion. Delivered contact and retention still require the unpowered fit check.
