Existing case: access and reusable mounts

Preparation instructions, September 8, 2026. The retained host purchase record identifies Fractal Design Meshify 3 XL. That establishes which manual to consult, not the current location of the radiator or any aftermarket restraints. Match the actual case to the illustrations before using these instructions.

Reference: Fractal Meshify 3 XL manual, V1.1. Page numbers below are both printed and PDF page numbers. They are manufacturer illustrations of an example build; the motherboard and GPU pictures are not our specific hardware.

Open access after shutdown

Use TRANSFER.md, step T1, first. Disconnect power and external cables, then protect the removed side panels. Page 12 shows the side-panel release motion; support a panel while releasing it and keep any glass panel on a padded surface away from the working area.

For top access, page 13 shows the outer panel sliding rearward before lifting. Leave the radiator carrier attached at this point. It may be carrying the full cooler assembly. Page 14 shows the carrier fasteners; opening the cosmetic panel does not mean those fasteners can also be removed immediately.

If the long internal cable cover obstructs access, page 15 shows its retaining screws, including a lower transport screw that may still be fitted. Check those before pulling on the cover. Pages 20–22 show cables passing through that area; free the appropriate connectors and restraints rather than dragging a plug through a passage while it remains connected at the other end.

The four existing GPUs and their actual slot latches still require the card-by-card procedure in TRANSFER T2. The case manual cannot show the added riser restraint or the ASRock latch hidden behind a particular card.

Remove a top-mounted radiator using its carrier

This sequence is conditional on finding the radiator on the top carrier. If it is mounted at the front or side, document that mounting before removing screws.

  1. Remove the GPUs and disconnect labeled cooler electrical leads. Leave the CPU cooler block fully attached to the motherboard.
  2. Arrange a stable padded support beside the case, within relaxed hose reach. Rehearse the carrier exit and subsequent motherboard movement before releasing fasteners. The radiator must be able to rest securely by itself.
  3. Support the radiator/carrier, release only its case retainers shown on manual page 14, and move it onto that support. Stop if a tube snags or the block/board is pulled. Do not detach the block to obtain extra reach.
  4. If needed, remove the short carrier screws with the radiator body supported. Save the carrier; the rack uses the XSPC stands. Keep fan screws separate.
  5. Transfer the motherboard per TRANSFER T4 with its cooler block still fitted and the radiator resting on its support. Reposition supports in short stages as needed; no loose radiator may hang from the connected motherboard.

Actual hose access and a stable landing arrangement must be established before releasing the last fastener. This is a handling plan, not a claim that the current case arrangement has been physically checked.

Remove the PSU with its factory plate still attached

Follow TRANSFER T6 only after its new support is ready. Label and disconnect the modular leads while keeping them assigned to their PSU. Verify no lead is still threaded around a fixed case part. Provide a stable support immediately behind the case at the PSU exit height; do not rely on the rear plate as a grip.

Reverse the installation in manual page 12: release the plate's case retainers, then slide the PSU rearward while supporting its body as it leaves the case. Keep the four plate-to-PSU screws fitted during withdrawal. Remove the plate on the workbench only if the final rack mount requires it. Never remove enclosure screws or open the supply.
