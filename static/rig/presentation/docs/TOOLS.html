Tools and consumables

Updated September 13, 2026. This is a reference checklist, not an order. The owner may already have ordinary tools and thinks the specialty tools are missing. Check capabilities before choosing any purchases. All 18 remaining supplement lines are deferred and excluded from the 26 ordered mechanical lines and subtotal. The old cart profile is unavailable; old receipts below are historical.

Keep the CPU cooler block attached throughout the transfer. No cold-plate removal, repasting or cooler-specific torque tools belong to this plan. The radiator remains connected by its sealed hoses and needs a stable separate support during handling. Do not carry either component by the hoses.

Start with metric Allen keys, suitable nut-holding tools and the frame's final torque capability. Reuse ordinary Phillips drivers and measuring tools already owned. The general checklist identifies sizes and jobs; its named premium models are examples, not requirements to buy duplicates.

Practical checklist — use what you already own

  • Metric hex keys: 2 mm for M3 low-head screws, 3 mm for M5 shelf screws, 4 mm for frame kits.
  • Nut drivers or small sockets: 5.5 mm for M3 nuts and 8 mm for M5 nuts; a thin-jaw adjustable wrench for the Harwin standoffs' 4.8 mm flats.
  • A torque screwdriver with a range covering 6.0 N·m and a compatible straight 4 mm hex bit/blade, for the extrusion joints only.
  • PH1 and PH2 Phillips screwdrivers for existing case/radiator hardware.
  • Metric ruler/tape, square, caliper, straightedge/level and two small padded clamps for the receiving and alignment checks.
  • Scissors, cutting mat, labels and small parts containers; a suitable ESD work surface and wrist strap for handling the electronics.
  • Loctite 222MS and SF 7649 activator, plus suitable metal-thread cleaning supplies, for the twelve specified switch/radiator nuts. These are consumables, separate from the tools. No purchase of them is recorded.

The XSPC radiator stand pair is already ordered; replacement PSU mounts are the remaining unselected mounts. No thermal paste or CPU-cooler torque tool is needed while the cooler block stays attached. Drilling tools are conditional on the shelf-fit fallback, not an automatic addition to this checklist.

FASTENERS.md now specifies the frame and metal-nut locking methods, including the selected IPA wipes for loose metal hardware. The frame torque tool does not cover every fastener in this table. Do not substitute pliers for a close-fitting nut tool or use an impact driver on the frame/PCBs.

The shelf-drilling fallback needs a drill, specified bits, backing/clamping, deburring tool, eye protection and complete swarf cleanup. Sizes follow the released shelf fastener plan. Perform it with the shelf removed and electronics well away; it is not part of assembling a populated rack.
