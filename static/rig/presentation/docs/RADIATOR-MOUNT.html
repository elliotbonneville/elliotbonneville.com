Radiator mounting — September 13, 2026

Mount the owned SilverStone XE360-TR5 upright across the front of the U11 host shelf on one pair of XSPC 120mm Universal Radstand V2 brackets. The feet extend under the radiator and its fans, toward the front of the shelf. They do not extend backward away from the radiator's weight. No extrusion changes or custom parts are needed. This completes the proposed radiator support and fastener arrangement; installation and delivered fit are not yet physically verified.

Parts and interfaces

Position and adjustability

The XSPC manufacturer drawing specifies 130 mm height, 60 mm width, 62 mm depth, 2 mm steel, 105 mm upright hole pitch, 5 mm upright holes, and 5 mm-wide base slots with 17 mm between their end centers. The two base slot rows are 30 mm apart. Bend radii and cosmetic details are simplified in the model.

The reference places the upright contact face at Z=265 mm, with feet extending to Z=325 mm, 25 mm behind the shelf front edge. Radiator and fans occupy Z=265–318 mm. Their nominal 394 mm width leaves 26 mm on each side of the 446 mm shelf. Brackets sit at the outer fan-hole columns; their separation is set by the actual radiator, not a custom crosspiece. Model hole-column positions and the un-dimensioned vertical hole datum are setup estimates, not machining coordinates. Both stands must sit flat when all four radiator screws are snug.

The four nominal shelf bolt centers are X=±167.5 mm and Z=280/310 mm: 55.5/390.5 mm from the left shelf edge and 70/40 mm back from the front edge. These are stand-slot positions, not measured shelf vents. Use the base slots and translate the whole radiator within its clearance to find suitable existing vents. Each washer must bear on sound steel on both sides of the opening. If four suitable positions cannot be found, use the already authorized shelf hole fallback: remove the shelf and electronics, mark through the actual stand, drill four 3.5 mm clearance holes on sound steel, deburr and remove all swarf. Do not drill the radiator, stand, or extrusion. Do not use the diagram as a drill jig. The kit is not dependent on a guessed perforation pattern.

Cooling and assembly

Keep the full CPU cooling loop: block, pump, radiator and three fans. Aim fan exhaust toward the open front (+Z), with air drawn from behind the radiator. The room fan supplements this cooler; it does not replace it. Orient the radiator with its hose end toward the CPU side and keep the radiator-integrated pump below the highest coolant-filled part of the loop; verify the delivered cooler's pump location before final tightening. Do not apply generic block-pump orientation advice to this radiator-pump cooler.

  1. With power disconnected, attach the two stands loosely to the bare radiator face using the four owned short case screws. Confirm free thread engagement.
  2. Place both feet flat on the shelf, underneath the radiator/fan mass. Snug the four mounting screws without twisting the radiator or pulling on its tubes.
  3. Trial the attached tubes to the seated CPU block before marking shelf holes. The original cooler has 460 mm tubes. Route smooth curves above/around the front host beam, clear of DIMMs, fans, sharp edges and connectors. The model retains approximate fittings: actual bend radius and hose fit must be checked.
  4. Mark the four foot positions, then remove the radiator from the stands for tool access. Use existing vents where suitable, otherwise the shelf-hole fallback above. Secure the empty stands first with all four shelf screws, upper/lower washers and nuts. The radiator/fans sit over these screw heads after installation, so do not plan to tighten them from above afterward. Reattach the radiator using the four short case screws from the rear. The feet carry the weight; bolts prevent movement. Never use tubes as restraints.
  5. Check fans turn freely and retain unobstructed intake/exhaust, then reconnect the pump and fans as directed by SilverStone. Its sheet requires stable 12 V at the pump header. Check pump/fan operation and CPU temperature on first boot.
