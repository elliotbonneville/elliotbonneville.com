Support the surplus on the shelf

The surplus rests in two padded areas along the GPU shelf's right edge (+X), away from leftward exhaust and outside the inter-card fan gaps. Rear group: G1–G4's eight data cables. Front group: G5–G8's eight cables. Host cables climb separately from U11, with their smaller surplus dressed along the supported route. The shelf carries the cable weight; loose reusable wraps organize and retain it. This replaces the unsupported elevated-loop proposal.

Coordinates are model setup references, not measured cable dimensions. Each bank clears native solids and modeled switch fan keepouts. The shelf surface is Y=799.875 mm; pads are nominally 1.5875 mm thick. See the repeatable space assessment.

Cut four 250 mm and four 60 mm lengths from the selected 12.7 mm-wide silicone stock. The longer strips support the sides of the cable bights; shorter cross strips protect the curved ends. Position them to suit the relaxed real cable, keeping all wire off sharp edges. The material allocation is 1,240 mm here, 320 mm for the eight GPU pads and 160 mm for four PSU far-end pads: 1,720 mm. Three 914.4 mm strips leave 1023.2 mm for leveling and installation spare.

Use stock reusable hook-and-loop cable wraps, such as VELCRO ONE-WRAP 90924, 8 × 1/2 inch, 50-pack. Allocate two loose wraps per device pair (16), two per host pair (4), and up to ten additional frame/route retainers; twenty remain spare. The wrap is a retainer, not a suspended weight-bearing cable tray. Do not wrap tightly around a PCB, connector boot or fan grille. Keep both plugs' latches accessible and put the same cable ID on both ends.

Assembly sequence and adjustment

  1. With all power disconnected, seat one supported device adapter's cable pair before the neighboring GPUs obstruct its underside latches.
  2. Route to its assigned switch with straight, relaxed exits behind both plugs. Form broad bights from the remaining length on the padded shelf; do not kink, sharply fold or cinch the twinax bundle. The bights need not match a mathematically exact oval or all have identical shapes.
  3. Secure each pair independently. Lift or release one pair without pulling on its neighbors. Keep the switch fans and GPU gaps open. Adjust the unloaded support rail height together if additional underside plug room is needed; preserve the aligned GPU/adapter support planes.
  4. Repeat, distributing bights along the two banks. Dress the four host cables separately around the shelf edge. Never pass a cable through an unverified sharp perforation or hold the shelf up with its connector.
  5. Before power-on, inspect the entire length of every cable, both latches, all fan paths and the ability to remove one card. If cable stiffness makes a bend resist the chosen route, spread/reposition the bight; do not force it into the rendered shape. The open-frame layout permits this adjustment.
