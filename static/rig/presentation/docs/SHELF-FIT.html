The old cad/out/gpu-shelf-fit.* files and shelf_fit_diagram.py output belong to the saved 4×2 manifest; their 365 mm span is superseded and must not be used for the row.

During empty-frame assembly, inspect the nearest vent centre, opening width/length, direction, local deck thickness and clear space below for the washer, nut and tool. Check both the bracket's upper bearing area and the lower washer's bearing area. All four direct host legs also need sound shelf contact under their 20 mm ends. A total shelf load rating does not establish a local vent's bearing strength.

Check the eight switch supports separately against their actual mounting holes and shelf vents. Use the selected stacks with supported existing openings or the authorized drilled-hole fallback.

The frames can translate only while preserving all attachment, card, cable and equipment clearances. Feet are tied to their posts; do not assume independent lateral adjustment at every foot. If existing vents cannot serve the measured positions, use the user's allowed drilling fallback, transferring the actual assembled foot bore to sound sheet. Do not order a custom bridge plate. Remove electronics before any drilling, deburr and clean, and never use this nominal drawing as the drilling template.

No pre-order shelf photos or measured vent map are required. Existing-hole attachment is preferred, but cannot be guaranteed from undimensioned perforations. Use the actual assembled mounts as the transfer reference. PSU brackets add eight M3 stacks; see PSU-MOUNT.md.
