I work at NVIDIA during the day as a software engineer, and fiddle with AI for fun on the side (when I can get away from my three toddlers).

This blog is where I write about things I find interesting - usually at the intersection of code, AI, and creative writing. I've been a professional software developer for eleven years, and writing fiction and non-fiction longer than that.

Another thing I'm thinking about a lot lately is how to work with AI as a creative partner, rather than letting it think for you.

## Why This Blog Exists

I wanted to write, and share my writing, but... I got tired of platforms. And social media. Medium wants to paywall your content. Substack owns your audience. Twitter limits your thoughts to soundbites and iteratively replaces them with brain rot.

So I built my own place on the internet. I think at this point it's the fourth iteration of my personal blog, and as usual for the newest thing I've built, it's the one I'm the most happy with. It's minimal, it's fast, and it'll outlive all those platforms.
